# design-system
- Use the Serene Paws design system: light cream background (#fbf9f8), teal primary (#0c5252), warm gray on-surface (#1b1c1c), Plus Jakarta Sans typography, rounded corners (sm: 4px, md: 12px, lg: 16px, xl: 24px), and soft shadows tinted with primary teal. Confidence: 0.85
